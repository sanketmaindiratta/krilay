How Auri works for you

A single, guided experience — from first hello to CRM-ready lead.

Think of Auri as the conversation layer for your website: one place where every visitor journey is understood, structured and handed over to your team with zero guesswork.

1

Understands what the visitor really wants

Auri asks smart, natural questions to uncover intent, budget, timing and key constraints — the things your reps normally spend the first 10 minutes extracting on a call.

2

Guides them through options like a human

Instead of throwing FAQs at people, Auri narrows choices, explains trade-offs and matches them to the right product, plan or service based on your playbook.

3

Sends your team a complete, concise summary

Before your team ever talks to the prospect, they see a clear summary: who they are, what they were trying to do, what they’ve seen, and what they’re likely to say yes to next.

The sales wishlist

Everything your team keeps asking for — in one place.

We spoke with sales and marketing teams who live in CRMs and spreadsheets all day. Auri is built to remove the most common “I wish we had…” pain points.

“I wish I knew who is actually serious.”

Auri scores each conversation based on intent signals: urgency, problem clarity, budget, and decision-maker presence. You see which conversations deserve a fast response, and which can be nurtured.

“Give me context, not just a form submit.”

Instead of one-line “message” fields, Auri hands over structured, narrative context: what they tried, what blocked them, and what they hope your team will do.

“Please don’t add another tool to learn.”

Auri works quietly in the background. Leads, notes and scores can appear directly in your CRM or inbox — no additional dashboards unless you want them.
